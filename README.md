# Sales-and-Inventory-Performance-Dashboard

1. Project Title (Headline)
Sales & Inventory Performance Dashboard | Power BI | End-to-End BI Solution

2. Short Description / Purpose
This dashboard provides an end-to-end analysis of sales and inventory performance across multiple dimensions such as time, product, country, and distribution channels. It is designed to help businesses monitor revenue trends, optimize inventory levels, and identify high and low-performing products for better operational and strategic decision-making.

3. Tech Stack
Power BI (Dashboard Development & Visualization)
DAX (KPIs, YoY Growth, Aggregations)
Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
Data Modeling (Star Schema)

4. Data Source
Sample retail / sales dataset (simulated business data)
Includes sales transactions, inventory levels, product details, and regional data

5. Features / Highlights
*Executive Summary
Total Sales: 2.68B
Total Quantity: 1M
Total Inventory: 38K
YoY Growth: 87.79%

*Sales Analysis
Year-on-Year sales trend (2025 vs 2026)
Sales distribution by country (Singapore vs Malaysia)
Time-based filtering (Year, Month)

*Product Performance
Top 10 products by sales
Bottom 10 products by sales (loss/low performers)
Pareto analysis (Top contributing products)
Average sales per product

*Inventory Insights
Inventory distribution by country and chain
Monthly comparison: Sales vs Inventory
Top 10 products with highest inventory

*Interactivity
Dynamic filters (Year, Month, Chain, Country)
Multi-page navigation (Summary, Product, Inventory)
Drill-down enabled visuals for deeper analysis

6. Key Insights
Significant YoY growth (~87%) indicates strong business expansion
A small group of products contributes to the majority of sales (Pareto principle)
Certain products have high inventory but lower sales, indicating potential overstock risk
Singapore contributes a higher share of total sales compared to Malaysia
Inventory distribution across chains suggests optimization opportunities
